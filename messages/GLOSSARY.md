# BuyMeATee translation glossary (ADR-019)

Terminology reference for all locales. Used by translators (human or AI) and
reviewers so product terms stay consistent. The brand name **BuyMeATee is
never translated** in any language. "Buy me a tee" as a phrase is only ever
translated where it reads naturally — never literally word-for-word in UI
copy; prefer the locale's natural equivalent of "support me / buy me a tee
(golf tee, not T-shirt!)". The tee is a **golf tee** — beware the T-shirt
false friend in every language.

Voice: supportive, trustworthy, warm but professional about money and fees,
golf-aware, never childish, never "donation/charity" framing.

Notes per language:
- **de**: Sie-Form ist NICHT gewünscht — freundliches "du" passt zur Marke (informal creator-economy tone). "Tee" needs the golf sense; avoid "T-Shirt" confusion by context.
- **fr**: tutoiement (tu) for creators-facing UI, vouvoiement acceptable in legal/payment copy. Keep "tee" as golf anglicism (universally used in French golf).
- **es**: neutral peninsular Spanish, tuteo. Golf vocabulary uses English loanwords (tee, green fee) as is standard.
- **it**: informal tu. Golf anglicisms standard (tee, green fee, caddie).
- **ja**: ですます調 (polite form) throughout; katakana loanwords for golf/creator-economy terms are natural. No spaces around interpolations unless natural.
- **ko**: 해요체 (polite informal) for UI; 합쇼체 acceptable for legal/payment. Golf loanwords in hangul standard.
- **pt**: European Portuguese (pt-PT) conventions — "tu"/informal but conjugations and vocabulary must be PT-PT (e.g. "Registar" not "Registrar" alone, "utilizador" not "usuário"). pt-BR may be added later as its own locale.
- All: numbers/currency/dates are formatted by code (Intl) — never hand-format in translations; keep ICU placeholders exactly as in English.
